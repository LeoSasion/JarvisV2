# Community conduct

JarvisV2 welcomes technically rigorous, respectful collaboration.

Participants must:

- discuss people and ideas without harassment or personal attacks;
- provide reproducible evidence for safety and compatibility claims;
- respect privacy and never publish credentials, dumps or personal data;
- disclose copied or derived work and preserve license attribution;
- avoid pressuring maintainers or users to bypass recovery or activation gates.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these rules,
and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or
users' systems.

Safety disagreements should be resolved with the more conservative,
fail-closed behavior until evidence supports a narrower boundary.
